## Class I - English

### 1. Listen & Do Adventure
**Skill Area:** Following Instructions & Announcements  
**Game Type:** Sequence Order  
**Duration:** 11.75 seconds

#### Action Instructions (Steps to Follow):
1. Open your book to page five
2. Draw a small blue star next to the heading
3. Read the three sentences aloud
4. Circle the picture of a red bus
5. Close your book and wait for the bell

#### Audio Script:
"Attention Class I! Please listen carefully. First, open your book to page five. Next, draw a small blue star next to the heading. Then read the three sentences aloud. After that, circle the picture of a red bus. Last, close your book and wait for the bell."

#### Questions from Audio:

**Q1: What must you do first?**
- Option A: Draw a blue star

- Option C: Circle the bus
- **Option D: Open the book to page five** ✅

## What's Needed for Each Listening Activity

### For Action-Based Activities (Following Instructions):
1. ✅ **Audio script** - Clear instructions for students to follow
2. ✅ **Action steps** - Visual list of steps to perform
3. ✅ **Questions** - Questions about what they heard
4. ❌ **Action images** - Visual demonstrations of each step
5. ❌ **Answer images** - Pictures showing correct vs incorrect actions

### For Comprehension Activities:
1. ✅ **Audio story/content** - The audio to listen to
2. ✅ **Questions** - Comprehension questions
3. ❌ **Story images** - Visual illustrations of the story
4. ❌ **Answer images** - Pictures for each option
