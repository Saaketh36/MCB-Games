## Class I - Listen & Do Adventure

### Video Scenario: Morning School Routine
**Duration:** 12 seconds  
**Setting:** Classroom with teacher and students

#### Video Script & Actions:

```
[SCENE 1 - 0:00-2:00] Teacher standing with class
AUDIO: "Good morning Class I! Listen carefully to my instructions."

[SCENE 2 - 2:00-3:30] Teacher opens book
ACTION: Teacher picks up a colorful book and opens it
AUDIO: "First, open your book to page five."

[SCENE 3 - 3:30-5:00] Student follows instruction
ACTION: Student opens book to show page 5 with pictures
AUDIO: "Next, draw a small blue star next to the heading."

[SCENE 4 - 5:00-6:30] Student draws blue star
ACTION: Student draws a blue star with pencil near the title
AUDIO: "Then, read the three sentences aloud."

[SCENE 5 - 6:30-8:00] Student reading
ACTION: Student reading sentences from the book aloud
AUDIO: "After that, circle the picture of a red bus."

[SCENE 6 - 8:00-10:00] Student circles bus
ACTION: Student circles a red bus picture in the book with marker
AUDIO: "Finally, close your book and wait for the bell."

[SCENE 7 - 10:00-12:00] Completion
ACTION: Student closes book, sits quietly, teacher rings bell
AUDIO: "Great job, everyone!"
```

#### Question 2: Hair Safety
**Question:** "Why should you tie back your long hair in the lab?"

**Visual Description:**
```
IMAGE: Before/After comparison
- BEFORE: Long hair down, dangerous near flames
- AFTER: Hair tied back with band, safe
- Bunsen burner in background showing heat
```

**Options:**
- A) To look neat ❌
- B) To keep it clean ❌
- C) To prevent it catching fire ✅
- D) Because teacher said so ❌

**Explanation:** Long hair can catch fire near heat sources. Safety requires tying it back.

---

#### Question 3: Rolling Up Sleeves
**Question:** "What is one reason to roll up your sleeves in the lab?"

**Visual Description:**
```
IMAGE: Rolled vs unrolled sleeves
- LEFT: Loose sleeves with fraying edges
- RIGHT: Sleeves rolled up neatly to elbow
- Hazard symbols around loose sleeves
- Safe symbols around rolled sleeves
```

**Options:**
- A) To show your arms ❌
- B) To prevent catching chemical spills ✅
- C) Because it looks cool ❌
- D) The teacher prefers it ❌

**Explanation:** Rolled sleeves prevent them from dragging through chemicals or catching fire.

---

#### Question 4: Lab Coat Purpose
**Question:** "What does the lab coat protect you from?"

**Visual Description:**

```
- Chemical splashes shown bouncing off

- Safety symbols on coat
```

**Options:**
- A) Keeping warm ❌
- B) Chemical splashes and spills ✅
- C) Looking like a scientist ❌
- D) Comfort ❌

**Explanation:** Lab coats protect clothing and skin from chemical spills and stains.

#### Question 5: Hand Washing
