# ✅ Simplified Sequence Question Guide

## What Student Sees (SIMPLIFIED)

Instead of drag-and-drop, students now see:

```
📍 Click ✓ to mark the CORRECT ORDER (1st, then 2nd, then 3rd...)

○ 💧 Wet your hands
○ 🧼 Rub soap  
○ 🫧 Rub for a count of 20
○ 💦 Wash off the soap
○ 🧻 Dry your hands
```

## How Student Answers

### **Step 1: Mark 1st Item**
Click on: "💧 Wet your hands"
```
✓ 1. 💧 Wet your hands  ← Marked!
○ 🧼 Rub soap
○ 🫧 Rub for a count of 20
○ 💦 Wash off the soap
○ 🧻 Dry your hands
```

### **Step 2: Mark 2nd Item**
Click on: "🧼 Rub soap"
```
✓ 1. 💧 Wet your hands
✓ 2. 🧼 Rub soap  ← Now marked!
○ 🫧 Rub for a count of 20
○ 💦 Wash off the soap
○ 🧻 Dry your hands
```

### **Step 3: Mark 3rd Item**
Click on: "🫧 Rub for a count of 20"
```
✓ 1. 💧 Wet your hands
✓ 2. 🧼 Rub soap
✓ 3. 🫧 Rub for a count of 20  ← Now marked!
○ 💦 Wash off the soap
○ 🧻 Dry your hands
```

**Continue for 4th, 5th...**

## Visual Feedback

- **○** (empty) = Not marked yet
- **✓** (check) = Marked as correct
- **Green highlight** = Selected item shows it's correct
- **Green border** = Visual confirmation

## How Scoring Works

✅ **CORRECT**: If student marked items in right order
❌ **WRONG**: If student marked items in wrong order

## Example Complete Answer

```
Q: Put steps of getting ready for school in order

✓ 1. 🛏️ Wake up
✓ 2. 🪥 Brush your teeth
✓ 3. 👕 Wear your uniform
✓ 4. 🎒 Pack your bag
✓ 5. 🚶 Go to school

System checks: Are they in CORRECT ORDER?
If YES → ✅ Correct
If NO → ❌ Wrong
```
