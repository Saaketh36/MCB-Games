## 🏗️ ACTIVITY 3: PLACE VALUE TOWER (MATH)

### ❓ Question 1: In the number 34, how many TENS?
**Answer: 3 TENS** ✓

### 📊 How many students got this RIGHT?

```
14 Students Got It Right!    ✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓✓
4 Students Guessed 4         ✗✗✗✗
2 Students Got It Wrong      ✗✗

Score: 14 out of 20 = 70%
```

**What they answered:**
- 14 said 3 TENS ✓
- 4 said 4 TENS ✗
- 2 said other numbers ✗

### 💡 Why this is correct:
**34 = 3 TENS + 4 ONES**
- The FIRST number (3) = TENS
- The SECOND number (4) = ONES

### 💰 Real Money Example:
**34 rupees = ?**
```
🔟🔟🔟  +  🪙🪙🪙🪙
  3 tens     4 ones
  (₹30)      (₹4)
             = ₹34
```

### 💪 This is a HARD topic!
70% got it right! Keep practicing! You'll get better! 💪

```

🍉 WATERMELON
🍉🍉🍉🍉🍉
= 5 students like watermelon
```

### 💡 How to Read This:
- **COUNT the fruits!**
- The fruit with the MOST items = The FAVORITE!
- 🍊 Orange has 16 items = MOST!
- 🍎 Apple has 13 items
- 🍌 Banana has 8 items
- 🍉 Watermelon has 5 items

### Answer: **ORANGE is the favorite!** 🍊

### 🏫 Why this matters:
Schools use this data to decide what to serve in the cafeteria!
If oranges are the favorite → They buy MORE oranges!
