🏗️ Activity 3: Place Value Tower
Mathematics - Numbers | Understanding tens and ones
Medium
❓ In the number 34, how many TENS do we have?
✓ Option A: 3 tens
Option B: 4 tens
Option C: 34 tens
Option D: 7 tens
💡 Why This is Correct:
34 = 3 TENS + 4 ONES. The first number (3) tells us the tens!
