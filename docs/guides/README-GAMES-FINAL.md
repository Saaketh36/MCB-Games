## 👨‍🏫 **FOR TEACHERS: 5-STEP ASSIGNMENT**

1. **Click "TEACHER"** button at top
2. **Select Path**: Class I → Subject → Skill → Game Name
3. **Enter Student Info**:
   - Student Name (text input)
   - Roll Number (text input)
4. **Set Deadline** (date picker)
5. **Click "ASSIGN"** button

✅ Game is now assigned and saved!

### **Track Student Progress:**
- Scroll down to "Track Results" table
- See Status: ⏳ Pending or ✅ Completed
- See Score: 10/10, 7/10, or 5/10 points
- Click "REFRESH" to update

---

## 👨‍🎓 **FOR STUDENTS: 2-STEP GAMEPLAY**

1. **Click "STUDENT"** button at top
2. **See assigned games** (teacher must assign first)
3. **Click "PLAY"** on any game
4. **Play the game** and try to get it correct
5. **Automatic save** - Status updates to ✅ Completed

✅ **Important**: Students can ONLY see games their teacher assigned!

## 🎮 **THE 5 GAMES EXPLAINED**

### **1️⃣ Listen & Do Adventure** 📖
**How it works:**
- Shows a story: "Open your book to page five..."
- Shows 5 shuffled action steps
- Student drags steps into correct order
- Checks answer
- Plays sound + animation
- Saves score (10/7/5 points)

**Example sequence:**
1. 📖 Open your book to page five
2. ⭐ Draw a small blue star
3. 📖 Read the three sentences aloud
4. 🚌 Circle the picture of a red bus
5. 📚 Close your book and wait

---

### **2️⃣ Story Time Quiz** 📚
**How it works:**
- Shows a story about Bunny and Nutkin
- Shows 5 multiple choice questions
- Student clicks answer
- Shows correct/wrong with color coding
- Displays score (10 pts per correct)

**Example questions:**
- What was the rabbit's name? (Options: Bunny, Nutkin, Butterfly, Oak)
- What color was the butterfly? (Options: Red, Blue, Yellow, Green)

---

### **3️⃣ Count & Match** 🔢
**How it works:**
- Shows "Count: 3"
- Shows 4 boxes with different numbers of circles
- Student clicks the box with 3 circles
- Correct answer = 10 points
- 5 rounds = 50 points total

**Examples:**
- Count: 2 → Click box with 2 circles
- Count: 5 → Click box with 5 circles

---

### **4️⃣ Simple Addition** ➕
**How it works:**
- Shows math problem: "3 + 2 = ?"
- Shows 4 multiple choice answers
- Student clicks correct answer
- Shows feedback with colors
- Score: 10 points per correct
- 5 problems = 50 points total

**Examples:**
- 2 + 1 = ? (Options: 2, 3, 4, 5)
- 5 + 3 = ? (Options: 7, 8, 9, 10)

---

### **5️⃣ Shape Sorter** 🔷
**How it works:**
- Shows 9 shape emojis on left (circles, squares, triangles)
- Shows 3 category boxes on right
- Student drags shapes into correct categories
- Checks if all correct
- Score: 10 points per category (30 total)

**Categories:**
- Circles: 🔴 🟡 🟠
- Squares: 🟥 🟦 🟪
- Triangles: 🔺 🔻
